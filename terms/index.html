Estimates are estimates

Next period dates, phases, the estimated ovulation, the fertile window, temperature shifts, patterns and every other prediction in the app are calculated from what you log and, if you allow it, from Apple Health, using the rules described in How estimates work. They can be wrong. Cycles vary, and illness, stress, travel, pregnancy, breastfeeding, hormonal contraception, medical conditions, perimenopause and plain old chance can all change them. Sensor readings from other devices and apps are shown as they arrive and aren't checked for accuracy.
